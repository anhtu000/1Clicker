## Title

Cosmic RNG Clicker

## Short description

Tap the core, scan the sky and merge Stardust all the way up to a Galaxy. Every scan and every merge rolls a rarity, from Common to Mythic.

## Description

Cosmic RNG Clicker is a space clicker with merging and rarity rolls. Tap the stellar core for energy, spend it on scans to find celestial bodies, and drag two of the same kind together to make the next one: Stardust, Meteoroid, Asteroid, Comet, Moon, Planet, Gas Giant, Star, Black Hole and, at the end of the chain, a Galaxy.

Every scan and every merge rolls a rarity: Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary or Mythic. The odds are always on screen, and a pity counter guarantees a rare find when luck runs dry. Rarer bodies on the board make every tap and every probe stronger, so a lucky roll speeds up the whole run.

Features:
- 10 stages and 5 rarities: 50 bodies to collect
- Some Galaxies hide a secret when they meet another world: 4 hidden bodies to discover
- 12 upgrades, from Core Focus and Probe Fleet to Auto Scanner and Gravity Well
- Shooting stars to catch, some of them golden
- Probes keep earning while you are away
- Timed runs: race from Stardust to a Galaxy and beat your 5 best times
- A daily gift and a decor shop to dress up your corner of space
- Plays on desktop and mobile, in portrait or landscape

## Controls

Desktop:
- Left click the core or press Space: collect energy
- Click Scan: find a new celestial body
- Drag a body onto a matching one: merge them
- Drag a body onto the core: turn it into energy
- Click an upgrade: buy it
- Click a shooting star: catch it

Mobile:
- Tap the core: collect energy
- Tap Scan, upgrades and shooting stars
- Drag a body onto a matching one to merge, or onto the core for energy
